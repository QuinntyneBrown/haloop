Foundation · Language

Content

Calm, plain-language copy that respects privacy, names actions clearly and guides recovery when things go offline.

Overview

Haloop's voice is calm, grounded and encouraging — like a steady training partner who respects your privacy and your time. Copy explains what happens on-device in plain language without AI hype or guilt.

Writing rules

  • Sentence case everywhere: headings, navigation links, buttons, tabs and table headers ("Morning strength & mobility", "Remove model", "Enable Chrome AI").
  • Verb-first actions: name the outcome ("Download", "Pair device", "Finish workout", "Remove model"). Never "OK", "Submit" or "Yes".
  • Numbers & units: use numerals with a space before units (94 MB, 134 bpm, 28 min, 5.2 km) and tabular figures.
  • Error formula: state what happened, why, and the immediate next step ("Couldn't connect to Polar H10. Wake the strap and try pairing again.").
  • Empty-state formula: clear title + one helpful sentence + one primary action ("No workouts logged yet. Start a guided session with on-device rep counting.").
