Foundation · Geometry

Shape

Five stepped radii plus pill/full and two border widths matched to control and surface size.

6 radii8 tokens
  • Overview
  • Radius scale
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Corner radius scales with container size: compact controls use --radius-sm or --radius-md, cards and dialogs use --radius-lg, and Haloop's signature Download pills, status badges and camera HUD pills use --radius-full.

Radius scale

--radius-xs
· Checkboxes, tab indicator
--radius-sm
· Inline code, kbd, tooltips, menu items, skeleton bars
--radius-md
· Buttons, inputs, wells, alerts, toasts, menus
--radius-lg
· Cards, verse cards, tables, dialogs (desktop), camera stage
--radius-xl
· Mobile bottom sheets
--radius-full
· Pill buttons, badges, chips, avatars, progress tracks, switches

Usage rules

  • Keep nested corners harmonious: a --radius-md control inside a --radius-lg card with --space-6 padding keeps visual alignment.
  • Use --border-width-hairline (1px) for cards, dividers and inputs; use --border-width-thick (2px) for focus rings and active tab indicators.
