Foundation · Depth & layering

Elevation

Four shadow levels, surface lightness progression and a strict nine-layer z-index scale.

4 shadow levels15 tokens
  • Overview
  • Shadows
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Elevation combines subtle shadows with surface lightness. In light theme, soft ink-tinted shadows lift cards and dialogs off warm paper; in dark theme, surface lightness steps upward (--color-bg-canvas → --color-bg-surface → --color-bg-surface-raised) alongside deeper shadows.

Shadow levels

Shadow 1 — CardsResting cards and primary button hover--shadow-1
Shadow 2 — Raised & tooltipsInteractive card hover, tooltips--shadow-2
Shadow 3 — Menus & toastsDropdown menus, popovers, toasts, skip link--shadow-3
Shadow 4 — Modals & drawersDialogs and slide-over sheets--shadow-4

Z-order & dark theme rules

  • Never write a raw z-index in component CSS; pick one of the nine --z-* layers.
  • Dark theme surfaces rely on surface lightness + hairline borders first, shadow second, so raised panels stay legible on deep ink.
