Card

Overview

Cards group related information on --color-bg-surface with a hairline border and --shadow-1. Haloop extends cards with .verse-card (3px left Halo gold accent border for daily Scripture) and .well (sunken surface for code or hardware callouts).

Anatomy

  1. Card surface--color-bg-surface, --radius-lg, --space-6 padding.
  2. HeaderTitle + optional status badge or action.
  3. Footer (optional)Separated by top hairline border.

Variants

Default card

Standard elevated surface for Today and Settings.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width inside responsive grids; --space-6 internal padding remains comfortable on 360px phones.

Theming

Uses component tokens --card-bg, --card-border, --card-radius and --card-padding.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Semantic <section> or <article>; clickable cards contain a single real link or button rather than making a <div> interactive.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to interactive controls inside the card.

Focus

Focus lands on the card's link or button.

Labelling

Use heading hierarchy (h2/h3) for card titles.

Contrast

Body text on card
Muted text on card

Motion

Interactive hover lifts 1px and transitions shadow over --duration-fast.

Touch

Generous padding separates controls inside cards.

Content

  • Keep card titles short; reserve .verse-card for Scripture quotes and reflections.
