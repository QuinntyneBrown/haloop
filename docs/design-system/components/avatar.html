Overview

Avatars use --color-accent-subtle and --color-fg-accent for initials, scaling cleanly across small (32px), medium (40px) and large (56px) sizes.

Anatomy

  1. CircleFull pill radius with accent-subtle fill.
  2. Initials or image2 uppercase letters or object-fit: cover photo.
