Content & tone

Haloop's calm, encouraging voice, sentence case, verb-first actions and reusable microcopy.

WCAG 2.2 AAPattern

Overview

Haloop speaks like a grounded training partner: clear about on-device privacy, honest about browser capabilities, and encouraging without hype.

When to use

  • Apply sentence case to every heading, tab, nav link and button.
  • Lead buttons with verbs naming the concrete result.
  • Use plain language for WebGPU, WASM and Gemini Nano states.

Structure

“All models run locally in your browser. No workout video leaves your device.”

Haloop privacy principle

States

Every state (empty, offline, error, busy) maintains the same steady, respectful tone.

Responsive behaviour

Keep button labels ≤ 3 words so they never wrap at 360px.

Accessibility

Link text and button labels make sense out of context for screen-reader users (WCAG 2.4.4).
