Empty & error states

First-run, filtered zero-results, offline and unsupported-browser recovery patterns.

WCAG 2.2 AAPattern

  • Structure
  • States

Overview

Haloop treats empty, offline and error states as helpful signposts rather than dead ends, always explaining what still works locally on-device.

When to use

  • First-run empty: explain the feature and offer one primary creation/pairing CTA.
  • Filtered no-results: show the active query and offer a "Clear filters" button.
  • Recoverable error: explain what failed and provide "Retry".
  • Offline / Unsupported: full-page recovery with clear capability summary.

Structure

No matching sessions

No workouts matched “intervals”.

States

Distinguish first-run empty from zero-results and from errors using distinct icons and copy.

Responsive behaviour

Centered stack caps prose at 36ch so lines stay balanced from 360px to 1280px.

Accessibility

Maintain heading hierarchy and give the recovery button a clear accessible name.

Content

Follow the 3-part formula: clear title + one calm sentence + one verb-first button.
